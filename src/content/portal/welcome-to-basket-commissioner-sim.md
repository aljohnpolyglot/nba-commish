# A Free Basketball Sim Built On Great Foundations

Basket Commissioner Sim is built for the kind of player who can lose hours in franchise mode.

At its core, this project stands on the shoulders of **Basketball GM** and dumbmatter's work. The roster ecosystem, long-save DNA, and browser-sim spirit matter here.

On top of that foundation, the direction pushes toward something more stylized and more mode-heavy, with inspiration from **NBA 2K** presentation and **Football Manager** save obsession.

## What you are actually getting

This is not just a schedule simulator.

You are stepping into a basketball world with:

- full-league commissioner control
- one-team GM careers
- fictional and modded league starts
- long offseason decision chains
- draft, free agency, training, coaching, and finance layers
- a save structure built for multi-season history

The main hook is simple: every year leaves scars, momentum, and unfinished business for the next one.

## Why this feels bigger than a plain browser sim

The pitch is not that this invented basketball sims from nothing.

The pitch is that it tries to turn strong open basketball-sim foundations into a **more complete free basketball universe**:

- more commissioner fantasy
- more front-office flavor
- more offseason structure
- more mode identity
- more "one more season" pull

If Basketball GM is part of your reference point, that is not something to hide. It is part of the lineage.

## Why the save loop matters

A good basketball sim does not peak in the first hour.

It gets stronger when:

- a young core becomes expensive
- a contender ages at the wrong time
- a rival keeps blocking your path
- one draft class changes the conference
- the league starts feeling like it remembers what happened before

That long-memory feeling is the reason people stay in a save for years.

## What kind of player this is for

This fits best if you want one of two fantasies:

### Run one team like it matters

GM mode is for roster builders, cap jugglers, and people who want every playoff exit to feel personal.

### Run the whole league like a sandbox

Commissioner mode is for the player who wants to bend format, shape stories, override outcomes, and push the entire basketball world in a direction.

## The best way to approach it

Do not try to master every mechanic on the first night.

Pick one save. Pick one role. Let one season teach you the rhythm. Once the calendar starts making sense, the rest of the game opens up fast.
