# Your First Save Should Be About Rhythm, Not Mastery

The best first save is not the most ambitious one. It is the one that teaches you how the year flows.

## Step 1: Pick the fantasy first

Before you even think about settings, ask what you want the save to feel like.

### Choose GM mode if you want pressure

GM mode is about one organization, one roster timeline, one cap sheet, one set of bets.

It is the better first choice if you want the franchise-mode feeling.

### Choose Commissioner mode if you want range

Commissioner mode is wider. You can shape rules, control league direction, and treat the whole save like a giant basketball sandbox.

It is better if you like experimenting more than roleplaying one front office.

## Step 2: Pick a league start you will actually enjoy

### Fictional start

Choose this if you want a clean new universe with no outside baggage. It is a strong pick if you care more about discovery than real-world familiarity.

### Modded start

Choose this if you want a world that feels more recognizable from the jump. It is easier to get emotionally attached when the frame already feels familiar.

## Step 3: Pick the right simulator for the job

### Realistic

Use this when you care about how games feel. It is stronger for watched games, playoff series, rotation checking, and seasons where you want texture.

### Fast

Use this when you care about throughput. It is better for commissioner saves, long skips, and pushing a universe forward quickly.

The simple rule is this:

- **Realistic** for immersion
- **Fast** for volume

## Step 4: Set one story goal

The save gets better immediately when it has a purpose.

Good first-save goals:

- win one title in three years
- rescue a weak roster without tanking forever
- build around one young star
- turn a fictional team into a powerhouse
- create a league history worth reading back later

## Step 5: Let the first season teach you the calendar

Your first year should answer:

- when the major checkpoints hit
- how the offseason changes tempo
- how the sim behaves when you go fast versus when you slow down
- what kind of team-building decisions you enjoy most

Do that, and the second season becomes the real start of the addiction.
