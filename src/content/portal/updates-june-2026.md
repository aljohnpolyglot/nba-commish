# Updates

This is the player-facing changelog for Basket Commissioner Sim.

No raw commit language. No file lists. Just what changed in the game and why it matters if you are actually playing long saves.

## June 2026

### H-O-R-S-E is now part of All-Star Weekend

All-Star Weekend got another real event instead of just a placeholder checkbox.

You can now run a full **H-O-R-S-E** competition with:

- adjustable contestant count
- repeat-shot rules
- live viewing flow
- a proper winner and result view

Why it matters:

All-Star Weekend keeps getting closer to feeling like an actual midseason event block instead of a quick menu stop between regular season games.

### The tutorial and article hub is now live

The landing experience now has a player-facing `Learn` portal instead of dumping everything into repo-style documentation.

Why it matters:

Players should not have to read dev docs to understand what the sim is, how the modes differ, or why the save loop hits.

## Late May 2026

### Euro saves got much stronger

A lot of recent work went into making Euro careers feel more coherent and less half-connected.

That includes:

- better Spain to EuroLeague qualification flow
- clearer Euro offseason order
- better retirement review flow
- stronger player-facing summer copy

Why it matters:

If a mode exists, it should feel like a real mode. Euro careers are getting pushed in that direction.

### Staff retirement reviews were added

Retirements are no longer just silent background cleanup.

Now there is more visible structure around staff departures and offseason review, which gives saves more continuity between seasons.

Why it matters:

Long saves are more satisfying when key exits are acknowledged instead of disappearing into the background.

### Death timing became date-based

Player and staff deaths now happen on a more calendar-driven model rather than feeling like one blunt year-end sweep.

Why it matters:

This makes long saves feel less gamey and more like a world that moves on its own timeline.

### Euro offseason language was cleaned up

A lot of visible summer wording was rewritten so it reads more like game text and less like internal tool language.

Why it matters:

The offseason is one of the most important parts of a multi-season basketball sim. If the wording feels mechanical, the mode loses atmosphere fast.

## Mid May 2026

### PBA isolated mode kept moving forward

The PBA branch continued getting more proper structure, including:

- its own setup timeline feel
- better scoped views
- better mode-specific navigation
- free agent and standings adjustments for that environment

Why it matters:

Mode identity matters. A basketball sim gets stronger when different save types stop feeling like the same shell with renamed labels.

### Euro recovery work kept expanding

There was also a large push on the Euro side around:

- staff hiring flow
- owner and sponsor seeding
- transfer-market behavior
- early-career setup
- team-intel cleanup

Why it matters:

This is part of the larger project goal: make the sim feel more complete across different career paths, not only in the default NBA-style route.

### Elam and target-score support got deeper

Rule customization around game formats improved, including stronger handling for:

- timed games
- target-score games
- Elam-style finishes

Why it matters:

Commissioner mode becomes much more interesting when format changes are not just cosmetic.

## Early May 2026

### Fictional leagues got more serious

Fictional saves were hardened with better setup logic, clearer branding, and stronger separation from NBA-only assumptions.

Why it matters:

If fictional mode is going to exist, it should feel intentional, not like a side effect of the main path.

### Bird Rights and contract cleanup were improved

There was important underlying work around:

- Bird Rights surviving the right situations
- stale trade and tenure issues
- waived-player ghost contract cleanup

Why it matters:

These are the kinds of systems players may not see directly, but they absolutely feel when a long save starts drifting or breaking.

### Coaching and training depth expanded

Recent work also pushed harder on:

- defensive identity layers
- matchup planning
- team familiarity systems
- stronger training structure

Why it matters:

The game is trying to be more than a click-through season sim. These systems are part of making team identity feel earned over time.

## The through line

The project keeps moving in the same direction:

- more long-save strength
- more mode identity
- more offseason depth
- more commissioner fantasy
- more player-facing polish

The goal is not to pretend this came from nowhere.

The goal is to take the browser-sim DNA that already works, respect the Basketball GM foundation, and push it toward a more complete free basketball sim with more style, more structure, and more reasons to stay in one save for years.
