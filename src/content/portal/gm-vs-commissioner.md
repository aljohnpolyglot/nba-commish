# Two Modes, Two Kinds of Basketball Power

Basket Commissioner Sim supports two very different ways to play, and the difference is bigger than a menu toggle.

## GM Mode: one franchise, real consequences

GM mode is the closer cousin to classic franchise mode.

You are responsible for one team and you live with what that means:

- roster construction
- trade timing
- draft decisions
- cap pressure
- free agency choices
- whether a title window is real or just hope

This mode works best if you want your save to feel personal.

One bad contract matters more. One breakout matters more. One conference finals loss lingers longer.

## Commissioner Mode: the whole league is your toy box

Commissioner mode changes the fantasy completely.

You are not just reacting to one franchise. You are steering the ecosystem:

- league rules
- format changes
- full-league balance
- story outcomes
- custom decisions that ripple across every team

This is the mode for players who want to push the world around and see what happens.

## Which mode should you start with?

### Start with GM mode if you want

- the most direct team attachment
- a cleaner learning curve
- a more traditional long-save challenge

### Start with Commissioner mode if you want

- maximum freedom
- more chaos
- more experimentation
- a basketball sandbox instead of a team-only career

## The real answer

Neither mode is better. They just serve different obsessions.

GM mode is for building a franchise.

Commissioner mode is for building a universe.
