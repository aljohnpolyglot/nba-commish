# Pick the World That Matches Your Brain

The first major choice is not your team. It is your universe.

## Fictional: a fresh basketball world

Fictional mode is for players who want the save to belong to itself from the first day.

You are not inheriting real-world expectations. You are not trying to preserve familiar narratives. You are building a league that can become strange, original, and fully its own.

This works especially well if you:

- love alternate histories
- care about discovery
- want to stop comparing everything to real basketball

## Modded: familiar frame, faster attachment

Modded mode gives you a world with more recognizable texture.

That matters because emotional attachment comes faster when the frame already feels grounded. Rivalries feel immediate. Rebuilds feel clearer. Storylines click sooner.

This works best if you:

- want a recognizable setup
- like remixing known basketball logic
- want quicker emotional buy-in

## Which one is better for a first save?

If you want your first save to feel easy to enter, pick **Modded**.

If you want your first save to feel like a new basketball universe from minute one, pick **Fictional**.

## The long-term difference

Fictional saves usually get weirder in a good way.

Modded saves usually hook faster.

That is the trade.
